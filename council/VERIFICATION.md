Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** (source supports it), **CONTRADICTED** (source says otherwise; the correct
value is given), **MISLEADING** (true but framed so it will mislead: wrong baseline, one favourable
point, missing caveat), **UNVERIFIED** (could not reach a source; say where you looked),
**ESTIMATE** (not checkable; method stated and reasonable, or not).

Snapshot time: I queried the DB at 2026-10-08 00:27 (+01:00). Dev server tests ran on http://localhost:3100 between about 00:30 and 00:40. Web sources were fetched on 2026-10-08.

- **Failed and load-bearing:** F13. The page times did not reproduce: home, markets and trending were 1.5 to 4 times slower, measured while many workers were running.
- **Failed, not load-bearing:** none.
- **True but misleading:**
  - **F3:** 0 of the top 100 traded tokens are scored. The 11.5% is all tail coverage.
  - **F7:** the Telegram conflict is probably Riven's own 13 duplicate workers (plus 6 duplicate perps scanners), not an outside bot.
  - **V5:** the real cut-off is about 04:59 WAT on 15 Oct (Phemex says 14 Oct), with rolling review. That leaves about 6 days, not 7.
  - **V2:** about 99% of indexed tokens are OTHER, not Argus or Tolly, and Tolly has 3 tokens.
- **Missing from the dossier (found while checking):** public eligibility rules require a project **deployed and functional on Arc Mainnet** and a **public code repository**. The repo's only remote is a local bare backup (F8, V7).
- **Could not reach (never reached, not verified absent):**

  - The written judging rubric.
  - Whether the swap encoder has been used with real money since 6 Oct.
  - F12 "on every page".
  - F9 "broadened today".
